01Are You Ready? A Readiness Checklist

ExamReady when you can…
Part 1 (Foundation)
  • Score 70% or more on Mock Exam 2 and 60% or more on Mock Exam 3
  • Name the objectives and key outputs of every ADM phase without notes (see Day 13)
  • Explain the difference between view and viewpoint, ABB and SBB, ADD and ARS, and compliant and conformant
  • List the Enterprise Continuum levels and the Architecture Repository components
Part 2 (Practitioner)
  • Score 24 or more out of 40 on today's paper
  • For any scenario, quickly identify the phase and find its steps and outputs in the standard
  • Explain why the 3-point and 1-point answers fall short, not just why the best one is right

03Exam-Day Tips

Part 1

  • 40 questions in 60 minutes: about 90 seconds each. Don't get stuck; return to hard questions at the end.
  • Watch for NOT, first, best and primarily, and be wary of absolutes such as "always" and "never".
  • Answer every question; there is no penalty for wrong answers.

Part 2

  • 8 scenarios in 90 minutes: about 11 minutes each. Read the question before the scenario.
  • Use the open book to check the phase's steps and outputs, but don't try to read whole chapters.
  • Rank all four answers. A sound second choice still earns 3 points.
  • Prefer answers that follow the ADM, involve stakeholders and respect governance over shortcuts.

04The Final Part 2 Mock Exam

The test below has eight scenarios: the Preliminary Phase, Phases A to G, and Requirements Management. It is scored like the real exam: 5, 3, 1 or 0 points per question, with a pass mark of 24 out of 40. Start the 90-minute timer, work through every scenario, then submit to see your score by phase and the reasoning for every answer.

The takeaway

  • Part 1 readiness: 70%+ on Mock Exam 2 and 60%+ on Mock Exam 3.
  • Part 2 readiness: 24/40 or more on today's paper, and the ability to explain every ranking.
  • Pace yourself: 90 seconds per Part 1 question, 11 minutes per Part 2 scenario.
  • Best answers follow the ADM, involve stakeholders and respect governance. Good luck!
