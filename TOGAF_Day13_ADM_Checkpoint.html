04The Most Common Exam Traps

  • Viewpoint vs view: the viewpoint is the convention or template; the view is what you actually produce.
  • Request vs Statement of Architecture Work: the Request triggers Phase A; the Statement is approved at its end.
  • ABB vs SBB: ABBs are vendor-neutral requirements; SBBs are product-aware implementations.
  • Phase E vs Phase F: E creates the initial roadmap and plan; F finalizes them.
  • Compliant vs Conformant: compliant = only spec features, but not all of them; conformant = all spec features, plus extras.
  • Requirements Management: it stores and supplies requirements, but the phases prioritize and address them.
  • Phase C order: Data and Application can be developed in either order.
  • TOGAF 10 structure: Fundamental Content plus Series Guides, not the TOGAF 9 "seven Parts".

The takeaway

  • The ADM runs Preliminary → A → B → C → D → E → F → G → H, with Requirements Management at the centre.
  • Phase A starts with a Request and ends with an approved Statement of Architecture Work.
  • Phases B–D share nine common steps and produce draft ADD, draft ARS and roadmap components.
  • Phase E creates; Phase F finalizes; Phase G governs; Phase H manages change.
  • Know which phase creates, finalizes and uses each deliverable: it's one of the most tested topics.
