1. About This Paper

This paper tests direct recall: definitions, the purpose of each phase, and the core facts every candidate must know. Most questions have one clearly correct answer. Use it to build confidence and find the topics you have not yet memorized.

Aim for 80% or more. Anything lower means the foundations need another pass before you try Exam 2.

All three mock exams follow the real TOGAF Enterprise Architecture Foundation (Part 1) format: 40 simple multiple-choice questions, 60 minutes, closed book, and a pass mark of 60% (24 correct). Each paper uses different questions, so you can take Mock Exam 2 and Mock Exam 3 as well without repeating yourself.

3. Exam Technique

  • Pace yourself: 60 minutes for 40 questions is 90 seconds each. Flag hard ones mentally and come back to them.
  • Read the stem twice. Watch for NOT, first, best and primarily.
  • Eliminate first. Distractors are often true statements about the wrong phase.
  • Be wary of absolutes such as "always", "never", "must" and "guarantees". TOGAF rarely speaks in absolutes.
  • Answer everything. There is no penalty for a wrong answer.
